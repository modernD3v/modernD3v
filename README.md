I've spent **15+ years** making complex things click for people — first as a music teacher and school administrator, then as a **software engineering instructor** building curriculum and guiding cohorts of aspiring engineers through full-stack development. Now I bring that same clarity to code.
 
I'm a **full-stack software engineer** with deep experience across the **JavaScript/TypeScript ecosystem** — React on the frontend, Node/Express on the backend, PostgreSQL for data — and a rare ability to communicate technical ideas with precision and empathy. Whether I'm architecting an API, writing documentation, or pair-programming with a teammate, I make the people around me better.
 
**I'm open to roles in software engineering, developer relations, and technical education.**
